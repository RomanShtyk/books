II OWNING YOUR CAREER

The single best career advice for any software engineer is:
**Own it!**

You own your career; don’t wait for your manager to drive it—set goals, create opportunities, and push your growth.

Be known for delivering real, impactful work (not busywork), and make sure others are aware of your results.

Keep a work log (“brag doc”) to track achievements, prioritize, and simplify reviews/promotions.

Actively seek and give specific feedback; it’s one of the fastest ways to improve.

Turn your manager into an ally: communicate, align on goals, and make their job easier.

Pace yourself: balance stretching (growth), executing (steady work), and avoid long periods of coasting or burnout.
